# Tachikoma Kit

Turns Sesame into a Ghost in the Shell Tachikoma. Frame, bottom cover, servos and electronics are stock.
Generated by the scripts in [hardware/cad/tachikoma](../../../cad/tachikoma/); clearances verified with `check_tachikoma_clearance.py`.

| Part | Qty | Colour | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top-Cover-Tachikoma-v200 | 1 | blue | Replaces the top cover. Print upright like the stock cover, with supports under the eyes/pod curve only. |
| Eyes-...-print-3-white | 3 | white | Press onto the eye pins (drop of CA if loose). |
| Lights-...-print-4-red | 4 | red | Press onto the stalk pins at the top front of the pod. |
| Arms-...-print-2 | 2 | grey | Press onto the pins under the screen. |
| L1/L2/R1/R2-Tachikoma-v100 | 1 each | blue | Hips with shoulder hump. Print on the flat outer face like stock. |
| Thigh-Shell-...-A / -B | 2 each | blue | Glue A onto L1 and R2, B onto L2 and R1 (flat face to the hip's outer face, knee ring around the two horn screws). |
| Knee-Cap-Tachikoma-v100 | 4 | white | Press into the knee ring; pops off to reach the horn screws. |
| L3/L4/R3/R4-Tachikoma-v100 | 1 each | blue | Feet with filled-in lower legs and recessed armour panels. Print lying on either flat side face, no supports. |

Press-in fit uses 0.15 mm clearance; if your printer runs tight, sand the pin lightly. Paint the head's skirt band dark grey.
Firmware: set `TACHIKOMA_FACES` in `sesame-firmware-main.ino` (on by default) for the Tachikoma eye faces.
